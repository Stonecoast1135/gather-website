## Image plan

Retain `launch-handoff.webp` for the recognizable Home hero and `launch-produce.webp` for the Impact metrics background. ## License verification

Each selected original photo page was opened and showed a free license. The [official Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) was reviewed during this pass. It permits free website and marketing use and modification; attribution is appreciated but not required. It forbids implied endorsement by pictured people or brands, offensive portrayal, stock-platform redistribution, and using the images as a trademark. No payment, subscription, or photographer outreach was needed or made. Credits are recorded below even though visible attribution is not required by this license.

Pictured people and businesses must remain illustrative. Do not turn these photographs into team portraits, testimonials, partner listings, or proof of completed Gather rescues. Generic “VOLUNTEER” clothing and food-package labels are present in some originals; no Gather branding was added. The van is an ordinary unbranded vehicle, not a claimed Gather vehicle. Small incidental product marks in the bakery and student photographs do not establish a partnership.

## Processing and visual review

Downloaded directly from Pexels' image service, then processed locally with Sharp. All outputs are WebP at quality 85 and effort 6, sRGB without unnecessary source metadata, and at most 1800 pixels on either side. No generative edits, fabricated branding, face edits, or inserted objects were used. Every original and final crop was visually inspected. The final eight files total 1,679,358 bytes (about 1.60 MiB); pages should continue using responsive `next/image` sizes and lazy loading below the fold.

The square teamwork and bakery crops deliberately retain extra vertical context so desktop and mobile can choose appropriate cover positions. Final in-page checks should preserve heads, working hands, and the food itself. Do not center-crop every photo indiscriminately or stretch source dimensions.

Rejected candidates are not included in the project: an otherwise suitable bakery view with prominent commercial signage; a very staged clothing-donation table; and a handcart scene whose visible contents did not establish food transport. The final transport selection more clearly matches the requested food journey.

Existing generated image provenance remains in [LAUNCH-ASSETS.md](./LAUNCH-ASSETS.md). This document covers only the eight newly sourced photographs and makes no claim about page test results.
