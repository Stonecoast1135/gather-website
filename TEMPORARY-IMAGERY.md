## Usage rules

- Treat every listed image as temporary editorial stock, never as evidence of Gather activity.
- Do not add Gather logos, uniforms, vehicles, boxes, partner marks, or captions that imply a real relationship.
- Preserve truthful alt text that describes the visible action without assigning identities.
- Record the photographer, license, approval, source URL, and intended crop when final imagery is supplied.
